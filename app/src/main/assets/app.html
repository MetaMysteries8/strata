BETTER CAPTURE, BETTER RESULTS

A little field guide.

01

Move through space.

Walk around the subject. Sideways motion and changes in height reveal depth; rotating in one spot does not.

02

Watch the real tracking points.

Start after tracking is stable. Textured, still surfaces make useful landmarks. Avoid mirrors, moving subjects, and rapidly changing light.

03

Cover the gaps.

Keep overlapping views and repeat the lap higher or lower. A good set of viewpoints matters more than standing still for longer.

04

Choose an output.

Splats reproduce how a scene looks. Meshes contain triangles for Blender, games, and printing workflows. A Gaussian PLY cannot simply be renamed to STL.

05

Keep the originals.

The dataset ZIP contains your photos, calibrated poses, and seed geometry. If training produces NaN values, retrain from those originals; an invalid PLY cannot restore the scene.
